Title: How to Report Abusive Behavior

We are committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users. If you experience or witness abusive behavior—including harassment, hate speech, threats, or any content that violates our Community Guidelines—please report it as soon as possible.

To report a user or piece of content, click the "Report" option available on profiles, messages, comments, or posts, and select the category that best describes the issue. Provide additional context where possible, such as screenshots, links, or a description of the interaction, as this helps our Trust & Safety team investigate efficiently.

Reports involving threats of violence, self-harm, or illegal activity are escalated immediately to our Trust & Safety team for urgent review, separate from standard support queues. All reports are reviewed confidentially, and the reported user will not be notified of who filed the report. Depending on severity, outcomes may include content removal, warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent account termination. You will receive a follow-up notification once your report has been reviewed.
