Title: How to Report a Security Vulnerability

We take the security of our platform seriously and encourage responsible disclosure of any vulnerabilities you discover. If you believe you have found a security issue, such as a way to bypass authentication, access another user's data, or execute unauthorized code, please report it immediately through our dedicated security channel rather than standard support.

To report a vulnerability, email security@example.com with a detailed description of the issue, including steps to reproduce it, affected URLs or endpoints, and any relevant screenshots or proof-of-concept code. Do not attempt to exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it, and do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to other users.

Our security team will acknowledge your report within 24 hours and provide updates as the issue is investigated and resolved. Verified critical vulnerabilities may be eligible for a reward under our bug bounty program. This report will be treated as a priority escalation and routed directly to our security engineering team.
